The grant

The £7,500 government grant, explained

The Boiler Upgrade Scheme (BUS) is a UK government grant that pays £7,500 toward an air source heat pump installation. We apply for it on your behalf — you don't deal with any paperwork.

Check if I qualify →

Who's eligible

  • You own your home (or you're a private landlord)
  • Your property is in England or Wales
  • You're replacing fossil fuel heating (gas, oil, LPG, or electric)
  • You have a valid Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) with no outstanding insulation recommendations

Typical costs for a 3-bed Cornish home

Full installation £11,000–£14,000
Government grant −£7,500
You pay £3,500–£6,500

Costs vary depending on your property, current radiators, and whether you need a new hot water cylinder. We'll give you an exact figure after the survey.

Air source heat pumps and solar PV. That's it. We don't fit gas boilers, we don't do bathrooms, we don't take on commercial work. Specialising means our engineers see the same systems every week and get very good at installing them.

We work locally.

Every member of our team lives in Cornwall or South Devon. Our vans are based in Truro. If something needs adjusting six months after install, we're 40 minutes away — not on a national call centre's queue.
